# Test 4 — Enchanted stars
Based on Test 3. Adds sparse world-direction star points with restrained shimmer
and rare short shooting-star streaks. Sky-only, night-only, weather-reduced.
Moon and aurora formulas unchanged. Offline GLSL validation passes.
In-game visual quality, meteor timing and frame pacing remain unverified.
Note: Test 3 cloud treatment fades at night, so realistic star occlusion by
nighttime clouds is not yet implemented. The shooting-star motion is a first
procedural experiment rather than a physically simulated meteor.
Keep Test 2 or 3 installed for comparison.
